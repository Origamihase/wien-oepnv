## Audit Update 14:08

### Affected Component: `docs/feed.en.xml` (Translation Pipeline)
**Display Issue Description:**
1. Clumsy translation of specific terms:
   - 'Laufveranstaltung' is translated as 'Running event' (e.g., 'Running event at 04.10.2027'). A better translation might be 'Marathon' or 'Running competition' depending on context, though 'Running event' is acceptable.
   - 'Busse halten' is translated as 'Buses stop' (e.g., 'Buses stop Salvatorianerplatz'). The literal translation sounds awkward because it's missing a preposition. It should be 'Buses stop at'.
   - 'Züge halten' is translated as 'Trains keep' (e.g., 'Trains keep in loop'). 'Halten' in this context means 'stop' or 'wait', not 'keep'.

**Proposed Solution:**
Update the `_GLOSSARY_BASE` in `src/build_feed.py` to map 'Busse halten' to 'Buses stop at' (or ensure 'halten' translates to 'stop' instead of 'keep'), and 'Züge halten' to 'Trains stop' or 'Trains terminate'.
