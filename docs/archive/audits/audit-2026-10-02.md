In the translated feed (`docs/feed.en.xml`), the placeholder protection leaked during the machine translation process. ### Proposed Solution
Update `_RESIDUAL_PLACEHOLDER_RE` in `src/build_feed.py` to account for bare index leaks like `0X`, `X0`, or similar patterns left by the MarianMT model when translating entity placeholders.
