## Audit Update: Wiener Linien Title Parsing

**Affected Component**
`src/providers/wl_lines.py` and/or `src/providers/wl_text.py` (Wiener Linien Provider)

**Display Issue Description**
There is a suboptimal parsing and presentation issue regarding multi-line disruptions. Currently, the feed generates titles like:
`<title><![CDATA[ 25: Linien 25 und 26 Betrieb ab Josef-Baumann-Gasse & Ersatzbus ab Josef-Baumann-Gasse ]]></title>`
The line extraction logic is flawed for compound line announcements. It extracts only the first line ("25: ") as a prefix and leaves the redundant phrase ("Linien 25 und 26") in the main title.

**Proposed Solution**
The parsing logic needs to be enhanced to detect multiple lines (e.g., matching "Linien X und Y" or "Linien X, Y und Z"). It should aggregate these into a clean prefix like "25/26: " and actively strip the redundant "Linien X und Y" string from the remainder of the title. The expected optimal output should be:
`"25/26: Betrieb ab Josef-Baumann-Gasse & Ersatzbus ab Josef-Baumann-Gasse"`
