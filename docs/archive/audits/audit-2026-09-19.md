## Audit Update: Wiener Linien Title Parsing

**Affected Component**
`src/providers/wl_lines.py` and/or `src/providers/wl_text.py` (Wiener Linien Provider)

**Display Issue Description**
There is a suboptimal parsing and presentation issue regarding multi-line disruptions. Currently, the feed generates titles like:
`<title><![CDATA[ 25: Linien 25 und 26 Betrieb ab Josef-Baumann-Gasse & Ersatzbus ab Josef-Baumann-Gasse ]]></title>`
The line extraction logic is flawed for compound line announcements. It extracts only the first line ("25: ") as a prefix and leaves the redundant phrase ("Linien 25 und 26") in the main title.

**Proposed Solution**
The parsing logic needs to be enhanced to detect multiple lines (e.g., matching "Linien X und Y" or "Linien X, Y und Z"). It should aggregate these into a clean prefix like "25/26: " and actively strip the redundant "Linien X und Y" string from the remainder of the title. The expected optimal output should be:
`"25/26: Betrieb ab Josef-Baumann-Gasse & Ersatzbus ab Josef-Baumann-Gasse"`

## Audit Update: Wiener Linien Redundant Line Enumeration

**Affected Component**
`src/providers/wl_lines.py` or `src/providers/wl_text.py` (Wiener Linien Provider)

**Display Issue Description**
There is another display issue regarding line extraction and title cleanup for the Wiener Linien provider. Currently, the feed generates titles like:
`<title><![CDATA[ 4A/80A/N29: 4A. 80A, N29: Wittelsbachstraße ]]></title>`
While the parsing logic successfully extracts the affected lines and creates a clean prefix ("4A/80A/N29: "), it fails to strip the corresponding raw enumeration from the rest of the title. Because the raw text uses inconsistent delimiters (e.g., a dot and a comma: "4A. 80A, N29:"), the standard string replacement or stripping regex misses it, resulting in duplicated and messy line information.

**Proposed Solution**
The title cleanup logic needs a more robust, delimiter-agnostic regex to detect and strip comma-, dot-, or space-separated lists of line codes from the beginning of the title text once the prefix is built. The expected optimal output should simply be:
`"4A/80A/N29: Wittelsbachstraße"`

## Audit Update 14:40: Wiener Linien Title Anomaly

**Affected Component**
`src/providers/wl_text.py` (`_tidy_title_wl`, `_AB_DATE_RE`)

**Display Issue Description**
The `_AB_DATE_RE` regex strips out "ab DD.MM." but strands 2-digit years (e.g. from "Bauarbeiten ab 14.09.26"). Since the 2-digit year is not matched and consumed by the optional `(?:\d{4})?` group, it remains in the string and is subsequently concatenated with preceding text (e.g. "Bauarbeiten26") after whitespace is collapsed. This leads to awkwardly concatenated strings in the output feed.

**Proposed Solution**
Update `_AB_DATE_RE` to account for 2-digit years by replacing `(?:\d{4})?` with `(?:\d{4}|\d{2})?` in the regex pattern to ensure the entire date string is matched and removed.

## Audit Update 20:19: OGD City of Vienna Compound Words
**Affected Component**
`src/utils/text.py` (`_GLUED_WORD_RE`, `repair_glued_words`)
**Display Issue Description**
The Baustellen OGD payload contains description fields with concatenated sentences and compound words lacking spaces, such as `DieHaltestelle`, `Derrechte`, and `DerFußgänger`. The current space insertion logic in `repair_glued_words` using `_GLUED_WORD_RE` relies on a strict list of allowed opening function words (e.g. `Im`, `Am`, `An`, etc.) but misses other standard German articles like `Der`, `Die`, and `Das` when they are followed by an uppercase letter.
**Proposed Solution**
Update the regex pattern `_GLUED_WORD_RE` in `src/utils/text.py` to include the missing German articles (`Der`, `Die`, `Das`) in the lookbehind enumeration. It should look like: `(?<=\b(?:Im|Am|An|Um|Ab|In|Zu|Es|Der|Die|Das))`. This will ensure proper space insertion for these incorrectly glued words in the OGD feed.
