# Audit Update: Feed Data Quality & Formatting

## Missing Spaces in Baustellen Descriptions
**Affected Component:** `src/providers/baustellen.py` (or `src/utils/text.py`)
**Display Issue Description:** The raw Baustellen JSON payload (e.g., `Märzstraße 49 bis Kreuzung Huglgasse...`) contains awkwardly concatenated sentences and compound words lacking spaces, such as `DerFußgängerverkehr` and `werden.Nähere Informationen`. While `repair_glued_words` exists in `src/utils/text.py` and is called in `src/build_feed.py`, it does not catch `DerFußgängerverkehr`, which causes the German and English feeds to inherit the glued words. This results in clumsy phrasing visible in both `docs/feed.xml` and `docs/feed.en.xml`.
**Proposed Solution:** Apply explicit regex spacing sanitization (e.g., updating the `_GLUED_WORD_RE` in `src/utils/text.py` to capture `DerFußgängerverkehr` by checking bounds, or applying it directly in `src/providers/baustellen.py`) to properly separate the glued words.

## Residual Placeholder Leak in English Translation
**Affected Component:** `src/build_feed.py` (English Translation Pipeline)
**Display Issue Description:** In `docs/feed.en.xml`, the translation of the track construction works for lines 46/49/52 includes a mangled entity placeholder: `in the area ofX Märzstraße`. The MarianMT model likely dropped the `XENT` or `XGLO` prefix and nonce, leaving a bare index leak `ofX`, which `_RESIDUAL_PLACEHOLDER_RE` in `src/build_feed.py` failed to account for.
**Proposed Solution:** Update the `_RESIDUAL_PLACEHOLDER_RE` regex in `src/build_feed.py` to account for bare index leaks (e.g., `X` or `ofX`) and ensure the translation pipeline correctly sanitizes residual mangled placeholders.

## Stranded 2-Digit Year in Title
**Affected Component:** `src/providers/wl_text.py`
**Display Issue Description:** The `_AB_DATE_RE` regex used by `_tidy_title_wl` has a flaw where the 4-digit year is optional, which can cause 2-digit years to be stranded and concatenated to the preceding word (e.g., transforming "Bauarbeiten ab 14.09.26" into "Bauarbeiten26"). The trailing `(?!\d)` guard prevents a partial consume on 3- or 5-digit years but allows 2-digit years to be misprocessed.
**Proposed Solution:** Correct the `_AB_DATE_RE` regex in `src/providers/wl_text.py` to ensure it properly consumes the 2-digit year without stranding it, preventing the concatenation of the 2-digit year to the preceding word.
