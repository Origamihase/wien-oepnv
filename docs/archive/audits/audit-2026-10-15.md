## Audit Update 16:30

### Affected Component: `src/build_feed.py` (Translation Pipeline / Glossary)
**Display Issue Description:** The German transit idiom "kann die Haltestelle [Name] nicht einhalten" (or "kann die Haltestelle [Name] nicht eingehalten werden") is translated clumsily or left literal in the English feed (e.g. for "1/2/5/5A/5B/71/74A/80A/D/O: Demonstration"). Because it is not explicitly mapped in the domain glossary, the literal translation creates awkward or confusing English text (e.g., "cannot follow the stop").
**Proposed Solution:** Update `_GLOSSARY_BASE` in `src/build_feed.py` to explicitly map the idioms `"kann die Haltestelle"` to `"will not be stopping at"` or `"nicht eingehalten werden"` to `"cannot be served"`.

### Affected Component: `src/utils/text.py` (Text Sanitization)
**Display Issue Description:** Raw payloads from "Stadt Wien – Baustellen" often contain glued words missing spaces (e.g., "DieHaltestelle", "Derrechte", "DerFußgängerverkehr"). The regex rules `_GLUED_WORD_RE` and `_GLUED_LOWER_RE` in `src/utils/text.py` fail to insert spaces between standard German articles ("Der", "Die", "Das") and the following word, leaving these glued words in the final output (e.g., `docs/feed.xml`).
**Proposed Solution:** Update `_GLUED_WORD_RE` or `_GLUED_LOWER_RE` in `src/utils/text.py` to include `Der`, `Die`, and `Das` as allowed opening function words for space insertion.

### Affected Component: `src/build_feed.py` (HTML Stripping)
**Display Issue Description:** In `src/build_feed.py`, when converting HTML descriptions to plaintext, block-level tags like `<h2>` and `<p>` are stripped out completely without substituting them with a space or line break. This can cause adjacent text fragments to concatenate awkwardly in the plain text `<description>` elements (e.g., creating sentences like "Bahnsteigsanierung Wegen").
**Proposed Solution:** Update the HTML stripping logic in `src/build_feed.py` to replace block-level tags (e.g., `<h2>`, `</h2>`, `<p>`, `</p>`, `<br>`) with spaces or line breaks before stripping the remaining HTML tags.
