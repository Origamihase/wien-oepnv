## Audit Update 14:35

**Affected Component**
`src/feed/glossary.py` and `docs/feed.en.xml` (Translation Pipeline)

**Display Issue Description**
The term 'Aufgelassen' is mapped to a capitalized 'Discontinued' in `_GLOSSARY_BASE`. This leads to mid-sentence capitalization errors in the generated English feed (`docs/feed.en.xml`) when lowercase variants (e.g., 'aufgelassen' or 'dauerhaft aufgelassen') are translated.

**Proposed Solution**
Adjust the glossary mappings to map the lowercase 'aufgelassen' to 'discontinued' while keeping 'Aufgelassen' as 'Discontinued' or relying on post-translation capitalization logic to handle it correctly without introducing mid-sentence capitalization.

**Affected Component**
`src/feed/glossary.py` and `docs/feed.en.xml` (Translation Pipeline)

**Display Issue Description**
The German term 'Gleiskörper' is missing from `_GLOSSARY_BASE`. This leads to mixed-language outputs in the English feed (as seen in the "26E: Carminweg" description where 'Gleiskörper' is left untranslated).

**Proposed Solution**
Add a mapping for 'Gleiskörper' (e.g., 'track area' or 'track bed') to `_GLOSSARY_BASE` in `src/feed/glossary.py`.

**Affected Component**
Frontend UI (`docs/assets/site.js`) and Statistics Generation (`scripts/generate_markdown_stats.py`)

**Display Issue Description**
There is a UI wording inconsistency: 'Minuten' is used in several places instead of the standard abbreviation 'min' (e.g., in `Verspätungen ⌀ Minuten je Wochentag` and `Letzte 60 Minuten`). Furthermore, `docs/assets/site.js` contains a logic mismatch, displaying `≥ 9 Minuten` in labels, whereas the backend logically applies strictly greater than (`> 9 min`).

**Proposed Solution**
Update labels in `docs/assets/site.js` and `scripts/generate_markdown_stats.py` to use 'min' instead of 'Minuten' consistently. Correct the `≥` symbol to `>` where it references the threshold logic.
