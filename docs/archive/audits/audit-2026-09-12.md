## Audit Update 14:21

### Affected Component
Backend (Translation pipeline `src/build_feed.py` and `docs/feed.en.xml`)

### Display Issue Description
The English translation of "Fahrtbehinderung" in the English feed (`docs/feed.en.xml`) results in clumsy phrasing. The German phrase "Nach einer Fahrtbehinderung kommt es zu unterschiedlichen Intervallen" is translated as "After an service obstruction there are different intervals." The glossary in `src/build_feed.py` translates "Fahrtbehinderung" as "service obstruction" (which begins with a consonant), but the NMT model translates "einer" as "an", expecting a vowel, leading to a grammatically broken article ("an service").

### Proposed Solution
In `_GLOSSARY_BASE` in `src/build_feed.py`, change `"Fahrtbehinderung": "service obstruction"` to `"Fahrtbehinderung": "obstruction"` (or similar vowel-starting noun, or simply "disruption" if NMT naturally handles the "a" vs "an"). "obstruction" cleanly pairs with "an".
